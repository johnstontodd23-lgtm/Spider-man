Setup
  1. Prop the phone up so its back camera sees the area you want watched (the door, the hallway, behind your chair).
  2. Keep the phone plugged in. The page keeps the screen awake while watching.
  3. Only direction and size of detected people and objects leave this phone. No video is sent anywhere.
  4. Nothing is reported on the first sighting. A person has to show up in three frames in a row, a weapon in two, before it counts. Dashed boxes are things the phone has seen once and is still deciding about.
  5. Knives, bats and scissors get a yellow box and a stronger alert on the headset. The detector catches a knife in plain view in good light; a knife in a moving hand across a room is often missed.
  6. A second detector looks for guns about twice a second once "Gun detector" says ready. In testing it caught a handgun within about 2 m of the phone in good light and missed it beyond that. Expect occasional misses and the odd false alarm on dark, gun-shaped objects.
