Real mode, step by step
  1. On the phone, open sentry.html at this same address, type the room code, press Start watching. Prop it up facing the area you want covered.
  2. Set Phone faces: "Away from me" if the phone's camera points the same way you're looking at it (watching the room beyond it). "Toward me" if its camera points back at you (watching behind you).
  3. Put the headset on, press Enter headset, then look straight at the phone and press Calibrate (or pull a controller trigger).
  4. When the phone sees a person, the ring lights up in their direction and you hear a ping. Closer is louder and faster. Distances are estimates from how tall the person looks to the phone.
  5. If the phone also sees a knife, bat or scissors, the ring turns yellow, the marker grows, the word on screen names the object, and a two-tone alarm sounds. Guns are checked by a second detector that works within about 2 m of the phone and misses beyond that.
